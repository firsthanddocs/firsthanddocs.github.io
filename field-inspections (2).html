Pricing

For national field-service companies (Safeguard, ServiceLink, Mueller, Seek Now), per-order rates are set by the platform. For direct clients — mortgage servicers, insurers, and local lenders — typical rates are:

  • Exterior inspection: $45–65
  • Interior / occupied inspection: $75–100
  • Commercial property: $100–150

Every job is quoted before we start — no surprises. Contact me with your property address and inspection type for a same-day quote.
