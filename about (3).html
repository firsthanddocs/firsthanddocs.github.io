Why Firsthand

I started Firsthand Document Services to bring something the big mail-in services and faceless vendors don't offer: a real local professional who handles your property and document work personally, and who you can actually pick up the phone and reach.

My background spans careers built on trust and attention to detail — years as a licensed marriage and family therapist, and years in mortgage lending as a loan officer. Both taught me the same lesson: when something matters to someone, the details and the follow-through are everything. That's the standard I bring to every inspection and every document.

Whether it's eyes on a property for a mortgage servicer, or an original document hand-carried to the Secretary of State, the work is done firsthand — carefully, reliably, and personally.
