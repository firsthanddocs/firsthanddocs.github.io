About

A trusted hand for important documents

I'm Justin, founder of Firsthand Document Services. After years of work as a Marriage and Family Therapist and as a mortgage loan officer, I built this business on a simple idea: the people handling your most important documents should be trustworthy, careful, and personally accountable.

Born and raised in Sacramento, I know the road to the Secretary of State well — and I made it my business to use that to your advantage. Ten years ago I moved my family to Morro Bay to put down roots on the Central Coast, and this community has been home ever since.

That's why I carry apostille documents to Sacramento myself, treat every client's paperwork like it's my own, and bring the same discretion to a living trust delivery that I'd want for my own family.
