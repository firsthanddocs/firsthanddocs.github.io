The everyday documents that need a witness

  • Powers of attorney
  • Advance health care directives

  • Contractor and vendor affidavits
  • Corporate resolutions

Personal & Travel

  • Affidavits and sworn statements

How It Works

Three steps, no surprises

1

Tell me what you have

Call, text, or send the form. Tell me the document, how many signers, and where you are. I confirm the fee and a time the same day.

2

I come to you

Signers present valid ID. I verify identity, witness the signature, complete the certificate, and record the act in my journal as California requires.

3

You keep your documents

Everything stays in your hands when I leave. Payment by card, tap-to-pay, or cash at the appointment.

Fees

Straightforward, and quoted up front

California sets the maximum fee for a notarial act at $15 per signature notarized. A mobile notary may also charge a separate travel fee, which is not set by the state and must be agreed to in advance.

My travel fee depends on distance and time of day. You'll have the full number before I commit to the appointment.

Attorneys, title companies, and firms with recurring volume: ask about standing appointments and flat monthly arrangements.
