How It Works

Three steps

  1. Tell me what you have. Document, how many signers, where you are.
  2. I come to you. ID checked, signature witnessed, certificate completed.
  3. You keep everything. Card, tap-to-pay, or cash at the appointment.
Fees

Two numbers

  1. $15 per signature. The state sets this maximum.
  2. A travel fee. Depends on distance and time of day. Agreed before I come.

Attorneys, title, escrow: ask about standing appointments and flat monthly rates.
