Powers of attorney, advance directives, and estate documents are the heart of what I do, often at a hospital bedside or in a care facility. Before this I spent years as a marriage and family therapist, which is what these appointments call for: patience, and a calm presence in a hard moment.

Fees

Two numbers, added together

$15 per signature notarized. California sets this maximum. Two signers on one document is two signatures. One signer on three documents is three signatures.

Plus a travel fee, based on where you are. This is separate from the signature fee and is agreed to before I book the appointment.

How It Works

Three steps

  1. Tell me what you have. Document, how many signers, where you are.
  2. I come to you. ID checked, signature witnessed, certificate completed.
  3. You keep everything. Card, tap-to-pay, or cash at the appointment.
Before I Arrive

Three things

  1. Every signer must be there. In person, no exceptions.
  2. Bring current photo ID. Issued within the last five years.
  3. Don't sign yet. Some acts require signing in front of me.
