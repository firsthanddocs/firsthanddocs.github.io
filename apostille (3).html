What is an apostille?

Certification for international use

An apostille certifies the authenticity of a public document so it can be legally recognized in another country under the Hague Convention. Non-Hague countries require a fuller authentication chain — both are handled.

Vital Records

Birth, marriage, and death certificates (certified copies) authenticated for use abroad.

Personal Documents

Powers of attorney, affidavits, single-status declarations, diplomas, and transcripts.

Business Documents

Corporate and export documents requiring authentication for international transactions.
